# Chat5H

## Setup
1. Put your GIPHY key in `GIPHY_API_KEY` at the bottom of the emoji/GIF section in `script.js` (developers.giphy.com).
2. In the Firebase console go to Firestore > Rules, paste in `firestore.rules`, and click Publish.
   Friends, DMs, calls and pasted photos will not work until the new rules are published.
3. Upload `index.html`, `script.js`, `style.css` and `firebase-config.js` to your host (calls need https).
